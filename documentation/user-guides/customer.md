# User Guide: Customers

## Signing Up

1. Open the Damazon site or install it to your home screen.
2. Choose a language: Arabic is the default, English is available from settings.
3. Tap **Create account** and enter your mobile number and email address.
   Syrian numbers in
   `09xxxxxxxx`, `+9639xxxxxxxx`, and spaced or hyphenated forms are all
   accepted.
4. Choose a password of at least eight characters.
5. Enter your name.
6. Open the single-use verification link sent to your email to finish creating
   the account.

Your number is your identity. Signing in later with the same number in any
accepted format reaches the same account.

## Signing In

Enter your number or email and your password. If you enter a wrong password
several times in a row the account is locked briefly; wait and try again.

## Finding What You Need

The home screen leads with the categories: restaurants, groceries,
supermarkets, pharmacies, and services. Use the search field to look for a
store, a restaurant, or a product by name.

- **Store pages** show the business rating, opening status, delivery zone
  coverage, and a promotion banner when one is running.
- **Restaurant pages** show the menu by section, with photos and preparation
  time.
- **Product pages** show images, description, price, and availability. Where a
  product has options such as size or variant, choose them before adding.

Prices appear in Syrian pounds with Latin digits, for example `125,000`.

## Choosing a Delivery Zone

Damazon divides each city into delivery zones. Before your first order you are
asked to pick your zone, and the available restaurants and stores change to match
it. You can save several zones, so a home address and a work address can both be
saved, and choose between them at checkout. Zone coverage also determines the
delivery fee and the expected time.

## Building a Cart

1. Add items from any store or restaurant.
2. Tap the cart icon to review. Items from different businesses cannot be in one
   order, so the cart separates them and you check out one business at a time.
3. Adjust quantities, remove items, and leave a note for the merchant or the
   restaurant if needed.

The cart is tied to your account, so it is the same on your phone and your
browser.

## Placing an Order

1. Choose a delivery zone, then a saved address or add a new one with a
   landmark.
2. Review the items, the delivery fee, and any discount.
3. Choose cash on delivery; it is currently the only available payment method.
4. Confirm. You receive a notification when the business accepts.

## Tracking an Order

Open **My Orders** and select an order to see its timeline:

| Stage            | What it means                      |
| ---------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Pending          | Waiting for the business to accept |
| Accepted         | The business confirmed and started |
| Preparing        | Being assembled or prepared        |
| Ready            | Ready for pickup by the courier    |
| Out for delivery | On the way to you                  |
| Delivered        | Completed                          |

The same timeline is shown in your notifications. You can cancel while the order
is still pending acceptance.

## Payment on Delivery

Pay the courier in cash when the order arrives. Keep the exact amount ready if
possible; change is not guaranteed.

## Reviews

After a delivered order you can rate the business and leave a comment. Ratings
become public on that business's page.

## Favorites

Mark a store, restaurant, or product as a favorite to find it quickly from the
Favorites screen.

## Notifications

Notifications cover order status changes, promotions, and account security
events. They appear in the app and, once you allow it, as browser notifications
on your device.

## Managing Your Account

Under **Profile** you can update your name, email, and preferred language, change
your password, manage saved addresses and delivery zones, and sign out of all
devices. Signing out of a device you no longer use protects your account.

## Support

Use **Support** to open a ticket about an order, a payment, or an account
problem. Include the order number and a short description; you will get a reply
in the ticket thread.
