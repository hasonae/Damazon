# User Guide: Businesses, Restaurants, Delivery, and Drivers

Damazon is one application with several modes. Your account holds the roles you
were approved for, and you switch between them from the profile screen. Switching
re-issues your session for that role, so what you see and can do changes
immediately.

## Switching Modes

Open the profile menu and choose a mode. If a mode is missing, the account has
not been approved for that role yet.

## Registration and Subscription

Submitting a merchant, restaurant, or delivery-company application does not
activate it. Damazon administration must review and approve the application.
After approval, the owner or an administrator pays the subscription in cash to
Damazon; an administrator records the cash received in the admin console.
Activation starts only after both approval and the recorded payment.

The subscription costs **500 new Syrian pounds (SYP) for 30 days**. It does not
renew automatically. Damazon sends an in-app reminder six days before expiry.
If it is not renewed, the business is suspended automatically at expiry and is
hidden from customers. The owner can still sign in and view the business status.
Visit Damazon administration and pay cash to reactivate; administrators can
also review the account and record another paid period in the console.

## Merchants

**Set up the shop.** Business name, description, logo, cover image, category, and
contact details. Upload the registration documents requested during approval.

**Zones and delivery.** Choose the delivery zones you cover and the delivery
service level you offer. Customers outside your zones will not see you.

**Catalog.** Create categories, then products. A product needs a name,
description, price, images, and stock. Where a product has variants such as size
or weight, define them and set stock per variant. Every stock movement is
recorded, so your on-hand quantity always reconciles with its history.

**Orders.** New orders appear in the Orders screen with a timer. Accept to start,
then move the order through preparation, mark it ready, and hand it to the
courier. Rejecting requires a reason, which the customer sees.

**Promotions.** Create coupons with a fixed or percentage discount, a minimum
order value, a usage limit, and an expiry date.

**Reviews.** Customer ratings appear on your shop page with the comment text.

**Payouts and invoices.** Available payouts, pending balances, and issued
invoices are listed under Finance. Amounts are in Syrian pounds.

**Team.** Add staff to your business and assign each person a role. A staff
member sees only your business's data, never another business's.

## Restaurants

Everything a merchant can do, plus the menu.

**Menu structure.** Organize the menu into sections such as starters, mains,
soups, and drinks. Each item has a name, description, price, photo, and
availability toggle.

**Ingredients.** Record the ingredients of each dish so the item can be marked
unavailable when an ingredient runs out. Ingredient-driven availability is what
keeps a restaurant from promising a dish it cannot make.

**Preparation and delivery.** Set preparation time, accept orders, and control
whether orders are accepted now or scheduled.

## Delivery Companies

**Fleet.** Add vehicles with a type and a plate number. Add drivers and assign
them to vehicles.

**Drivers.** Each driver has an availability status. Set a driver's status to
available or offline to control whether they receive tasks.

**Dispatch.** Tasks arrive according to your assignment mode: company dispatch,
where you assign a driver yourself, or driver acceptance, where nearby available
drivers pick up tasks. Open a task to see the pickup and drop-off details, then
assign it.

**Zones.** Confirm the zones your company operates in.

## Drivers

**Availability.** Toggle yourself available or offline. You only receive tasks
while available.

**Tasks.** Accept an offered task and navigate to the pickup point, then to the
customer. Update the task status as you go: picked up, on the way, delivered. The
customer sees these updates on their order timeline.

**Earnings.** Completed tasks and their amounts appear in your earnings history.

**Documents.** Submit the required vehicle and personal documents for verification.

## Rules That Apply to Every Business Mode

- Prices are entered in Syrian pounds with decimals and are stored exactly, so
  totals always reconcile.
- A business with a lapsed subscription or missing approval cannot publish and
  cannot receive new orders.
- Data you see is limited to your own business. Role and permissions are enforced
  by the platform, not by your interface.
- Support tickets are answered through the platform, not directly by customers.
