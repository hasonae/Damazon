Vision

Damazon is not merely an online shopping application.

It is a complete digital commerce ecosystem designed to become the central infrastructure for commerce, delivery, restaurant services, logistics, subscriptions, and digital payments within Syria.

The platform must be extensible to support future regional expansion without requiring architectural redesign.

Every component must therefore be modular, scalable, secure, observable, and independently maintainable.

The AI Agent must never generate code immediately after reading a feature request.
